Ayurclinic finalised direction

Patient master will be separate from case papers.

New case paper will be created by selecting an existing patient using patient_id.

Patient details for print will be picked from patient master automatically.

Case paper will contain visit-specific data only.

We will keep 4 boxes in the case paper:

Symptoms / complaints

Previous treatment / findings

Notes / advice

Prescription

Speech-to-text will be added to the note boxes.

Attachments like reports/X-rays/PDFs will be linked to the visit/case paper.

Screen layout will be compact:

left side = patient master panel

right side = 4 smaller scrollable boxes

Print layout will combine patient master + case paper content and keep the output clean and compact.
